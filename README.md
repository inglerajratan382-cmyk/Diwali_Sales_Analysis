Objective

To analyze Diwali sales data and identify key customer segments, purchasing behavior, and high-performing product categories to improve sales strategy and customer targeting.

Method

Performed data cleaning & preprocessing (handled null values, removed duplicates)
Used Pandas & NumPy for data manipulation
Conducted Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on:
Gender-wise spending
Age group analysis
State-wise sales distribution
Product category trends
Created visualizations using Matplotlib & Seaborn
Segmented customers based on demographics (age, gender, occupation)

Results 

Female customers contributed ~64% of total sales
Age group 26–35 accounted for ~38% of total revenue
Top 3 states (UP, Maharashtra, Karnataka) generated ~52% of sales
Highest spending customers were from IT, Healthcare, and Aviation sectors
Top categories:
Food (~25%)
Clothing (~22%)
Electronics (~18%)
